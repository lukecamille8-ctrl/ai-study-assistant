A simple study loop

From “where do I start?” to “I’ve got this.”

Make each session count with a repeatable rhythm that puts understanding first.

01 / FOCUS

Pick one thing to learn

Choose a topic, lesson, or set of notes. Start small and keep your goal clear.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Make the hard parts make sense

Explore a clear explanation, surface the key ideas, and follow your curiosity with questions.

03 / PRACTICE

Try it from memory

Check what you know with practice prompts, then revisit the parts that need another look.

04 / RETURN

Build momentum over time

Keep a manageable plan and come back for another focused session when you’re ready.
